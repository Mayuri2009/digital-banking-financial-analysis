## 2. Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders for the Digital Banking Financial Performance & Customer Profitability Analysis project.

It defines each stakeholder’s role, interest, influence, information needs, and engagement approach.

Since this is an independent portfolio project, the stakeholders and their responsibilities are simulated to reflect a realistic digital banking analytics environment.

## 4. Stakeholder Needs and Expectations

### 4.1 Finance Manager

**Primary needs**
- Understand monthly and annual revenue performance.
- Identify the contribution of different revenue sources.
- Monitor operating costs by category.
- Compare revenue and operating cost trends.
- Review financial KPIs using consistent USD ($) reporting.

**Expected outcome:** A reliable financial performance view that supports business review and investigation.

### 4.2 Head of Digital Banking

**Primary needs**
- Understand customer activity and transaction trends.
- Monitor customer acquisition and retention indicators.
- Identify changes in transaction performance.
- Understand which customer segments contribute most to revenue.

**Expected outcome:** A consolidated view of digital banking performance and potential growth opportunities.

### 4.3 Customer Analytics Manager

**Primary needs**
- Compare customer activity across segments.
- Analyze revenue contribution by customer segment.
- Understand acquisition channel performance.
- Explore customer retention patterns.

**Expected outcome:** Insights into customer behavior and segment-level performance.

### 4.4 Operations Manager

**Primary needs**
- Monitor transaction success and failure rates.
- Analyze transaction activity by type and period.
- Review customer support volume and performance.
- Identify areas that may require operational investigation.

**Expected outcome:** Better visibility into operational performance and potential inefficiencies.

### 4.5 Financial Analyst

**Primary needs**
- Define financial and operational KPIs.
- Perform revenue and cost analysis using PostgreSQL.
- Identify trends, variances, and potential business drivers.
- Develop evidence-based recommendations.
- Document assumptions and analytical limitations.

**Expected outcome:** Clear, traceable analysis that supports business decision-making.

### 4.6 BI Analyst

**Primary needs**
- Organize datasets into an analytical model.
- Develop SQL queries and reusable calculations.
- Create Power BI measures and dashboard visuals.
- Validate dashboard outputs against SQL results.

**Expected outcome:** An accurate, usable, and maintainable analytical reporting solution.

### 4.7 Project Sponsor

**Primary needs**
- Understand the project objectives and scope.
- Review the final dashboard and key findings.
- Assess whether the deliverables address the business problem.
- Receive concise recommendations supported by analysis.

**Expected outcome:** A completed portfolio solution demonstrating structured business and financial analysis.

## 7. Stakeholder Risks and Mitigation

| Risk ID | Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| DBFPA-R-01 | Stakeholders interpret revenue as customer profit | Misleading financial conclusions | Clearly distinguish revenue from profit and document cost allocation assumptions |
| DBFPA-R-02 | KPI definitions vary between stakeholders | Inconsistent reporting | Maintain a centralized KPI catalog |
| DBFPA-R-03 | Dashboard contains metrics that are difficult to interpret | Reduced usability | Use clear labels, definitions, and appropriate visualizations |
| DBFPA-R-04 | Data does not support a requested business question | Incomplete analysis | Document limitations and avoid unsupported conclusions |
| DBFPA-R-05 | Financial values are reported using inconsistent currency labels | Incorrect interpretation | Use USD ($) consistently throughout the project |

## 8. Stakeholder Engagement Approach

The project will follow a structured, iterative approach:

1. Identify stakeholder roles and information needs.
2. Translate business needs into requirements and KPIs.
3. Develop SQL analysis and dashboard components.
4. Review analytical outputs against stakeholder expectations.
5. Validate calculations and reconcile financial metrics.
6. Present findings and recommendations in a concise executive summary.

This engagement approach is simulated as part of an independent portfolio project and does not represent actual stakeholder participation at a financial institution.

## 9. Conclusion

The stakeholder analysis establishes the information needs and engagement approach for the Digital Banking Financial Performance & Customer Profitability Analysis project.

The Finance Manager, Head of Digital Banking, and Project Sponsor are treated as high-influence stakeholders. Their needs guide the financial KPIs, dashboard design, and final recommendations.
